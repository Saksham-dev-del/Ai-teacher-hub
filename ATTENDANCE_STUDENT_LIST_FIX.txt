ATTENDANCE STUDENT LIST FIX

Issue:
The Phase 8 attendance dropdown only listed students who had already attempted a quiz owned by the logged-in teacher. A newly registered student therefore remained invisible.

Fix:
- Attendance now lists every registered active account whose role is student.
- Attendance creation uses the same corrected access rule.
- The UI shows a clear message when no active student account exists.

After updating:
1. Restart the backend.
2. Sign in as teacher/admin.
3. Open Academic Suite > Attendance-Based Reminder.
4. The registered student should appear immediately.
